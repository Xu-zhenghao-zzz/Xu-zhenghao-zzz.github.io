Research

A selection of research projects I have worked on.

GRACE: Graph-Aware Routing Along with Compression for End-to-End Optimization

Sep. 2025 – Present

Fourth Author · IEEE TNSM, Major Revision

Implemented the PPO training and evaluation pipeline for joint routing and in-network compression with GNN-encoded network states.

Designed cross-topology generalization experiments by directly evaluating policies trained on one topology on unseen topologies. The manuscript received a Major Revision decision from IEEE TNSM; I am responsible for additional generalization, workload, and sensitivity experiments during the revision.

RobustVidBench: Benchmarking Robustness of Multimodal Video Understanding

Sep. 2025 – Present

Fourth Author · Manuscript in preparation

Worked on benchmark data and evaluation pipelines for video MLLM robustness.

Implemented video preprocessing and controlled degradations including bitrate/resolution/frame-rate reduction, frame dropping, stuttering, and audio-video misalignment. Also participated in QA verification, model evaluation, experimental analysis, and manuscript preparation.

Automated Video Understanding Dataset Construction

Feb. 2026 – Present

Fourth Author

Participated in the design and implementation of a Video → Knowledge Graph → Summary → QA data-construction pipeline.

Implemented core video-processing and QA-generation components and used vLLM for batched model inference. The overall project covers 600+ videos; I directly worked on approximately 150 videos and generated/processed nearly 10,000 QA samples across the main pipeline and experimental variants.

A Recurrent Rab8a-associated Hypoxic Niche in Spatial CRISPR Tumour Data

Jul. 2026 – Aug. 2026

First Author · 2026 PEBBLE BioFusion Workshop, Westlake University

Worked on a dual-view GNN approach for spatial CRISPR tumour data, combining local cellular-neighborhood information with gene-expression features. I was responsible for model implementation, experiments, and manuscript preparation as the first author of the workshop manuscript. The work received the Excellent Research Report Award.

Computation–Routing Joint Optimization in UAV Networks

2025 – Present

Second Author

Led problem formulation, reinforcement-learning implementation, and simulation experiments for joint routing, compute placement, and compression decisions in a shared wireless environment.
