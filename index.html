Research Interests

Reinforcement Learning & Structured Decision Making. I am interested in reinforcement learning for complex structured decision-making problems, including PPO-based policy optimization, state and action-space modeling, generalization evaluation, and robustness in unseen environments. Some of my previous work uses network topology and resource allocation as experimental settings for studying sequential and multi-variable decision making.

Graph Learning. I am interested in representation learning and decision making over graph-structured data, particularly using graph neural networks to encode structural relationships and combining graph representations with reinforcement learning policies.

Multimodal AI. I study multimodal foundation models, with a particular interest in dataset construction, robustness evaluation, and experimental analysis for video understanding models. My current work includes evaluating MLLMs under realistic video degradations, multimodal data processing, semi-automated annotation, and large-scale Video-QA construction.

LLM Agents & AI Systems. I am interested in LLM-based agents, retrieval-augmented generation, automated experimental design, and AI research workflows. I am currently exploring scalable LLM-agent pipelines for data generation and experimentation, as well as systematic evaluation of LLM behavior and reliability in complex experimental tasks.
