Research Experience

GRACE: Graph-Aware Routing Along with Compression for End-to-End Optimization

Sep. 2025 – Present

Fourth Author

Implemented the PPO training and evaluation pipeline for joint routing and in-network compression with GNN-encoded network states. Designed cross-topology generalization experiments by directly evaluating policies trained on one topology on unseen topologies, and conducted workload, parameter-sensitivity, baseline, and ablation experiments. The manuscript is currently under Major Revision at IEEE TNSM.

RobustVidBench: Benchmarking Robustness of Multimodal Video Understanding

Sep. 2025 – Present

Fourth Author

Worked on benchmark data and evaluation pipelines for video MLLM robustness. Implemented video preprocessing and controlled degradations including bitrate/resolution/frame-rate reduction, frame dropping, stuttering, and audio-video misalignment. Also participated in QA verification, model evaluation, experimental analysis, and manuscript preparation.

Automated Video Understanding Dataset Construction

Feb. 2026 – Present

Fourth Author

Participated in the design and implementation of a Video → Knowledge Graph → Summary → QA data-construction pipeline. Implemented core video-processing and QA-generation components and used vLLM for batched model inference. The overall project covers 600+ videos; I directly worked on approximately 150 videos and generated/processed nearly 10,000 QA samples across the main pipeline and experimental variants.

A Recurrent Rab8a-associated Hypoxic Niche in Spatial CRISPR Tumour Data

Jul. 2026 – Aug. 2026

First Author · 2026 PEBBLE BioFusion Workshop, Westlake University

Developed a dual-view representation approach for spatial CRISPR tumour data, combining local cellular-neighborhood information and gene-expression features with graph neural networks. Contributed to model implementation, experiments, and manuscript preparation. The work received the Excellent Research Report Award.

Computation–Routing Joint Optimization in UAV Networks

2025 – Present

Second Author

Led problem formulation, reinforcement-learning implementation, and simulation experiments for joint routing, compute placement, and compression decisions in a shared wireless environment.
